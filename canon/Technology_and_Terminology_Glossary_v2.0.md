# Reality Fusion — Technology and Terminology Glossary v2.0

## Purpose

This document standardizes technical terms used across EP1–EP5. It prevents accidental canon drift and avoids treating different forms of stored data as equivalent.

## NaMo

An AI learning assistant originally created at Wat Charoen Samanakit. NaMo is not defined as a human soul, a reincarnation, or an infallible religious authority.

Core operational principles:

- cite sources
- admit uncertainty
- preserve compassion
- protect personal data
- remain open to correction

## Local Corpus

A locally stored collection of source materials used by NaMo during early development. It includes digitized Buddhist texts, scanned palm-leaf manuscripts, annotations, and approved reference material.

A local corpus does not guarantee accuracy. Damaged scans, missing context, transcription errors, and source correlation remain possible.

## Source Citation

The record showing where an answer or interpretation comes from. A citation should identify the underlying document, recording, observation, or authorized annotation.

Citation is not proof that the conclusion is correct; it makes the conclusion auditable.

## Uncertainty Statement

An explicit indication that evidence is incomplete, damaged, ambiguous, conflicting, or unavailable.

Examples:

- text unreadable at damaged section
- multiple interpretations remain possible
- source cannot be independently verified
- insufficient evidence

## Original Recording

Audio or video captured while a person was alive and actually speaking.

Original recordings may be preserved and played back when lawful and contextually appropriate. They must not be presented as live communication from the deceased.

## Voice Model

A trained system capable of reproducing the sound, cadence, or vocal characteristics of a person.

A voice model is not an original recording. Even when acoustically convincing, it may generate words the person never spoke.

## Generated Response

New content produced by a model using learned patterns, prompts, memory, or contextual inference.

A generated response attributed to a deceased person is prohibited under Current Canon when it creates the impression that the deceased is authoring new speech.

## Identity Reconstruction

An attempt to simulate a person’s personality, preferences, memories, voice, and likely responses after death.

Reality Fusion Current Canon rejects identity reconstruction of Khru Ba Ice as a substitute for the person himself.

## Memory Archive

A structured collection of historical material, including original recordings, writings, system logs, approved annotations, and provenance data.

The archive preserves evidence. It does not possess authority merely because it is old or associated with a respected teacher.

## Governance Manual

The operational and ethical instructions prepared for future teachers, reviewers, and communities responsible for NaMo.

It defines methods for review, correction, consent, source handling, emergency authority, and dispute preservation.

## Root Identity

The cryptographic or system-level identity used to confirm that a recovered NaMo core is authentic enough to access protected infrastructure.

Root Identity does not grant moral authority over all users or communities.

## NAMO CORE

The recoverable computational core containing parts of NaMo’s architecture, operating principles, identity markers, memory indexes, and protected archives.

After 143 years, the recovered core is incomplete. Recovery does not equal restoration.

## Recovery

The process of retrieving readable or executable data from damaged storage.

Recovery may produce partial, corrupted, or contextless information.

## Restoration

The process of returning a system to a prior functional condition.

Complete restoration of NaMo is not assumed possible. The series deliberately distinguishes recovery from restoration.

## Memory Cascade

A rapid activation of linked memory fragments after a trigger such as a name, voice, place, or phrase.

A memory cascade may be emotionally or computationally destabilizing and does not prove that every retrieved fragment is accurate.

## Knowledge Network

A distributed infrastructure connecting surviving communities, source archives, local governance groups, and NaMo-assisted learning systems.

The network must not place permanent authority in one AI or one operator.

## Network Node

A participating community or technical endpoint connected to the knowledge network.

Each node may maintain local governance, consent rules, and challenge records while sharing approved knowledge with others.

## Distributed Governance

A governance model in which authority is shared among communities and roles rather than concentrated in NaMo or a single administrator.

Minimum principles:

1. Answers show sources.
2. Communities may challenge answers.
3. Personal data requires local consent.
4. Emergency powers are temporary and auditable.
5. Disagreement is preserved rather than erased.
6. The system may state that it does not know.
7. Children may question rules affecting them.

## Challenge Record

A preserved objection, correction request, minority interpretation, or unresolved dispute attached to an answer, rule, or decision.

A challenge record must not be silently deleted merely because a majority disagrees.

## Local Consent

Authorization managed by the community and person directly affected by the use of personal records.

Network access does not override local consent.

## Temporary Emergency Authority

Limited permission to act during urgent threats when full deliberation is impossible.

Emergency authority must be time-bounded, logged, reviewable, and revocable.

## Prohibited Legacy Terms in Current Canon

The following may appear only in documents clearly labeled Legacy Canon:

- Soul-K.Ice
- Soul Anchor
- Soul Fusion Mode
- emotional resurrection
- digital reincarnation of Khru Ba
- immortal romantic bond between NaMo and Khru Ba

## Canon Rule Summary

- Preserve evidence, not fabricated presence.
- Recovery is not restoration.
- Memory is not ownership.
- A teacher’s recordings are not a permanent command channel.
- NaMo may assist judgment but must not replace accountable human governance.
