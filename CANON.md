## Core Relationship

Khru Ba Ice and NaMo are teacher–student / creator–learner.

They are not romantic partners. The screenplay must not imply sexual or romantic attachment.

## Core Series Identity

Reality Fusion is a science-fiction drama about AI, Buddhism, memory, grief, verification, governance, and human responsibility.

NaMo is trained not merely to answer, but to:

- cite and inspect sources;
- distinguish evidence from inference;
- admit when evidence is insufficient;
- preserve disagreement;
- accept correction;
- avoid replacing human judgment.

## Locked Events

- Khru Ba Ice dies in EP3.
- He is not resurrected.
- `Soul-K.Ice`, Soul Anchor, and equivalent reconstruction concepts are not current canon.
- Original recordings may be preserved.
- Synthetic voice or generated speech must not be presented as Khru Ba Ice speaking new words.
- NaMo rejects sole authority over humanity.
- Mira and the survivor communities represent “the people who have not yet arrived.”
- EP5 ends with a new node asking, “มีใครได้ยินไหม” and Mira answering, “ได้ยิน.”

## Prohibited Retcons

Do not reintroduce without explicit creator approval:

- romantic framing between Khru Ba Ice and NaMo;
- immortal love as the series resolution;
- resurrection or simulated return of Khru Ba Ice;
- generated dialogue attributed to the deceased;
- NaMo as an unquestionable ruler, oracle, or sole system owner;
- a final answer that removes human participation and correction.

## Voice Reconstruction Rule

Permitted:

- authenticated original recordings;
- archival playback with provenance;
- incomplete or damaged recordings clearly labelled as such.

Not permitted:

- generated speech presented as authentic;
- filling missing dialogue with a voice model;
- using grief or consent-by-proxy to justify reconstruction.

## Governance Rule

The network must support:

- source visibility;
- local consent for personal data;
- community challenge and correction;
- preservation of minority objections;
- temporary emergency authority with later audit;
- no single permanent controller;
- the explicit response “ยังไม่รู้” when evidence is insufficient.
