# Security and Copyright Guidelines for Reality Fusion

To protect the intellectual property (IP) and ensure professional handling when approaching agencies or studios, follow these guidelines:

## Official Copyright Registration

- **Domestic:** Register the screenplay with the Thai Department of Intellectual Property (กรมทรัพย์สินทางปัญญา) to obtain an official timestamped record of authorship.
- **International:** For broader protection, register with the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) or other relevant international bodies to secure rights in multiple jurisdictions.

## Secure Storage of Files

- Maintain copies of all documents (PDF/DOCX) with timestamps and metadata in a trusted storage service (e.g., Google Drive/Dropbox) to serve as evidence of creation and modification.
- Use version control (e.g., Git or timestamped archive folders) to document changes over time.

## Non‑Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

- **Before sharing full materials,** require recipients (agents, producers, studios) to sign an NDA that prohibits disclosure, reproduction, or use of the IP without permission.
- The NDA should include definitions of confidential information, obligations, and duration. See the included NDA_Template for a basic structure.

## Option Agreement or Script Sale Agreement

- If a party expresses interest, prepare an **Option Agreement** (granting the right to develop/produce the work for a limited period) or a **Script Sale Agreement** (full transfer of rights). Clear terms should include purchase price, duration, rights granted, and credit.

## Creative Ownership Statement

- Include the following statement on or within all documents:  
  **“บทภาพยนตร์ Reality Fusion เป็นทรัพย์สินทางปัญญาของ Kanin Raksaraj. ห้ามใช้ ดัดแปลง หรือเผยแพร่โดยไม่ได้รับอนุญาตเป็นลายลักษณ์อักษร.”**
- This reinforces your legal claim and informs recipients of ownership.

## Watermark and Metadata

- Apply a subtle watermark (e.g., your name or company) on each page of critical documents (One‑Pager, Series Bible, Scripts) to deter unauthorized distribution.
- Embed metadata in each file (author, version, date) when exporting to PDF or DOCX.

## Tiered Sharing Strategy

- **First Round:** Provide only the Pitch Pack (Query Letter, Series Synopsis, Moodboard) to gauge interest.
- **Upon Request:** Share the Master Package (full scripts, bible, pitch deck, research) only after an NDA is signed or a serious inquiry is made.

## Recordkeeping

- Keep proof of all transmissions: store emails sent (export as PDF) and maintain logs of shared links (set to view‑only). This helps establish who received what and when.

By implementing these measures, you protect your intellectual property, demonstrate professionalism, and establish a clear process for potential partners.
