# REALITY FUSION — PITCH AND SUBMISSION READINESS GATE

Version: 1.0
Status: INTERNAL PACKAGE ASSEMBLED — NOT YET CLEARED FOR EXTERNAL SUBMISSION

## Purpose

This gate separates work completed in the repository from work that requires a human read, creator decision, qualified review, legal advice, or recipient-specific approval. It prevents the presence of a document from being mistaken for clearance to sell, finance, produce, or submit the project.

## 1. Completed internal evidence

- [x] Current-canon screenplay set identified: EP1 v2.1, EP2 v2.1, EP3 v2.2, EP4 v2.1, EP5 v2.1.
- [x] Five DOCX files and five PDF files generated and versioned in `deliverables/current-screenplays/`.
- [x] Package ZIP integrity, DOCX containers, and PDF readability checked.
- [x] English logline, synopsis, episode guide, creator statement, one-page pitch, and pitch-deck copy exist.
- [x] Buyer submission package index created.

## 2. Creator-owned decisions before outreach

- [ ] Approve the final buyer-facing title, creator credit, contact details, and copyright notice.
- [ ] Select the exact recipient and verify its live submission policy.
- [ ] Approve the exact materials to disclose to that recipient.
- [ ] Human-proofread the EP1 PDF and buyer-facing English materials after any final editorial change.
- [ ] Select or commission only properly licensed visual assets for a designed deck.

## 3. Production and finance materials

- [ ] Script breakdown: scene, cast, locations, props, stunts/safety, VFX, and technical requirements.
- [ ] Preliminary production plan and budget range prepared by an appropriately qualified producer or line producer.
- [ ] Format, intended market, financing ask, and use of funds approved by the creator.
- [ ] Table read and evidence-based runtime report completed; until then, runtime remains `PENDING EXTERNAL PROCESS`.

## 4. Rights and legal gate

- [ ] Confirm chain of title and the party authorized to offer rights.
- [ ] Obtain qualified counsel review of the ownership statement, NDA, option template, governing law, commercial terms, credits, and consultation terms.
- [ ] Clear or replace third-party images, fonts, music, trademarks, quotations, and other material used in any external deck or sample.
- [ ] Confirm the disclosure path for confidential materials and recipient terms before sending.

## 5. Content and specialist review gate

- [ ] Buddhist terminology and monastic-practice review by an appropriate qualified reviewer.
- [ ] Medical and palliative-care review where represented in the scripts.
- [ ] Child safeguarding review where represented in the scripts.
- [ ] Privacy, technology, synthetic-voice, and AI-governance review where represented in the scripts.

## 6. Decision rule

Use `PITCH PACKAGE ASSEMBLED` only when Section 1 is complete. Use `CLEARED FOR A SPECIFIC EXTERNAL APPROACH` only after the applicable items in Sections 2–5 are completed or the creator has explicitly recorded a conscious waiver with the recipient and limitation stated. Neither label means production-ready or guarantees financing, selection, or acceptance.
