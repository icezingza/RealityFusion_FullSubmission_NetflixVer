## Producer's required next pass

For each numbered scene, the appointed producer or line producer should record:

- cast and background performers;
- location / set / company move;
- day or night; interior or exterior; weather and control needs;
- props, picture vehicles, costumes, hair and make-up;
- animals, minors, stunts, water, weapons, smoke, electrical or other safety needs;
- practical effects, VFX, screen graphics, playback, archival-audio and sound needs;
- permissions, consultant dependencies and foreseeable legal / insurance questions;
- preliminary shooting time and cost drivers.

## Production conclusion

The project has a contained early-world base, a hospital sequence, a distinct off-world block, and a post-collapse block. Whether those blocks are affordable, consolidatable, or technically feasible is `PENDING PRODUCER ASSESSMENT`.
