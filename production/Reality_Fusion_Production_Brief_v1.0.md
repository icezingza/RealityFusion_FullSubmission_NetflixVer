# REALITY FUSION — PRODUCTION BRIEF

Version: 1.0
Status: CREATIVE / DEVELOPMENT BRIEF — NOT A BUDGET OR GREENLIGHT PLAN

## Project frame

- Format: five-episode limited series.
- Genre: grounded science-fiction drama with Thai Buddhist cultural setting and ethical-AI conflict.
- Current active scripts: EP1 v2.1, EP2 v2.1, EP3 v2.2, EP4 v2.1, EP5 v2.1.
- Current formatted package: five DOCX files and five PDF files in `deliverables/current-screenplays/`.

## Creative production intent

The visual approach should begin intimate and tactile: wood, rain, manuscripts, aging computer hardware, archival sound, and human labor. The later science-fiction scale should feel like an extension of those materials rather than a separate spectacle. The story depends on performance, silence, and source-recording boundaries as much as on visual effects.

## Production strategy questions

1. Can the temple, school, and hospital worlds be scheduled in a geographically efficient local block?
2. Can the off-world sequence be designed as a limited number of reusable controlled sets plus targeted VFX, instead of multiple large standalone environments?
3. Can the post-collapse community and ruined-city material share locations, builds, and dressing strategies?
4. What performance and sound design method will represent NaMo consistently without making claims of an actual person's generated posthumous speech?
5. What is the viable release / financing model after a producer has assessed market, language, casting, and delivery requirements?

## Budget posture

No budget range is stated here. Cost depends on decisions that have not been verified: country and union context, cast, episode duration, location access, practical-set scope, child-performer safeguards, VFX shot count, post-production workflow, insurance, and delivery specifications. A producer or line producer must create and own the estimate.

## Readiness requirements before a producer budget pass

- table-read evidence and runtime report;
- human proofread of the buyer-facing sample and deck;
- scene-level production stripboard from the current scripts;
- creator-approved visual references, cleared for external use;
- specialist-review plan for Buddhist practice, medical/palliative material, safeguarding, privacy, and synthetic voice / AI governance;
- chain-of-title and rights confirmation suitable for the intended conversation.

## Handoff request for an attached producer

Ask for a written response covering:

- budget tier options and assumptions;
- schedule outline and likely company moves;
- location-consolidation plan;
- VFX / practical-effect strategy and cost drivers;
- required department heads and consultants;
- legal, insurance, child-safeguarding and cultural-review dependencies;
- delivery risks and the information needed for a financing or commissioning discussion.
