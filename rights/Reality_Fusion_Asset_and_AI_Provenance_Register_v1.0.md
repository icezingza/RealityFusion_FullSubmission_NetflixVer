## Important separation

Composing a song, owning a particular recording, and granting a production the right to synchronize or distribute that music can involve separate rights and permissions. This register does not decide those questions.

## Information needed to complete the music entry

- exact writer / composer names and splits;
- lyricist, arranger, producer, performers and recording engineer, if any;
- whether a label, distributor, publisher, collaborator, sample, beat, loop, or commissioned contributor is involved;
- whether the MP4 contains a master recording, music video, visual footage, artwork, or third-party material;
- intended project use: reference only, pitch-deck playback, trailer, teaser, episode, festival, or commercial release;
- written authorization or counsel-reviewed agreement before it is included in an external package.

## Repository rule

Do not commit private master files, unreleased music, identity documents, contracts, or personal correspondence to this repository unless the creator explicitly approves disclosure and the relevant rights review is complete.
