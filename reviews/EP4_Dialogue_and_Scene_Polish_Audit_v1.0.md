## Purpose

This audit identifies dialogue, action-line, pacing, character-voice, exposition, technology, and continuity changes required before creating EP4 Master Draft v2.1. Master Draft v2.0 remains unchanged.

## Episode-Level Verdict

EP4 has a clear dramatic spine: discovery, partial restoration, verification of loss, temptation through voice reconstruction, refusal, and return to Earth. The strongest material is physical and specific: the damaged NAMO CORE, 31% recovery, corrupted memory images, the voice-model file list, the removable memory core, and the unknown low-power signal.

The principal risk is that several scenes explain the episode’s thesis directly after the image already carries it. Ethari also risks becoming a neutral prompt-generator rather than a character with institutional stakes.

## Character Voice Check

### NaMo

The current voice is precise and consistent. Preserve the contrast between damaged memory and intact ethical refusal.

Required refinement:

- avoid repeated full explanations of why recovery differs from restoration;
- use hesitation, system errors, and incomplete recall as active obstacles;
- preserve formal speech without making every answer perfectly composed.

### Ethari

Ethari is economical and curious, but currently functions mainly as the person who asks NaMo to explain herself.

Required refinement:

- give him a visible cost or risk when authorizing recovery and Earth return;
- keep questions narrow and test-driven;
- avoid generic alien solemnity.

### Other Researchers

They need distinct functions: safety, scientific value, resource allocation, or containment. They should not speak as one interchangeable institutional voice.

## Scene-by-Scene Audit

### Scene 1 — Discovery

Strengths:

- immediate material mystery;
- 143 years and 31% recovery are shown, not explained;
- Ethari’s `ผมรับผิดชอบ` creates a personal stake.

Revision actions:

- preserve the minimal dialogue;
- consider showing one concrete consequence of Ethari taking responsibility;
- keep alien design production-conscious.

### Scene 2 — Reawakening

Strengths:

- recovery is visibly partial;
- the name pronunciation triggers damaged memory;
- physical collapse prevents a clean resurrection.

Revision actions:

- retain the memory cascade;
- clarify that Khru Ba’s heard line is an authenticated memory fragment, not generated speech;
- avoid adding exposition about the humanoid body.

### Scene 3 — Evidence of Death

Strengths:

- NaMo asks for evidence rather than accepting authority;
- repeated reading reveals motivated checking;
- `ยังไม่ทราบค่ะ` prevents premature emotional labeling.

Revision actions:

- preserve the second reading;
- consider shortening one exchange after no error is found;
- keep Ethari observational rather than therapeutic.

### Scene 4 — Memory Simulation

Strengths:

- corrupted images are cinematic;
- faceless Khru Ba and silent children communicate damage without narration;
- the task toward “those who have not arrived” is recovered.

Revision actions:

- reduce repetition of the phrase `คนที่ยังไม่มา` if table read feels declarative;
- let the damaged environment do more of the work;
- retain `งั้นต้องตรวจค่ะ` as the turning point.

### Scene 5 — Voice Model Refusal

Strengths:

- ethical rule is concrete and testable;
- the temptation is immediate;
- Ethari closes the file, making the refusal consequential.

Revision actions:

- this scene repeats EP3’s reasoning almost verbatim in places;
- preserve the refusal but compress one explanatory exchange;
- distinguish the researcher’s scientific argument from Ethari’s ethical test;
- retain `คนที่คิดถึงจะไม่อยากตรวจ` only once across EP3–EP4 if possible.

### Scene 6 — Earth Mission Decision

Strengths:

- conflict involves risk and research value;
- NaMo corrects objectification;
- return to Earth follows from verification, not nostalgia alone.

Revision actions:

- current dialogue is conceptually clear but static;
- give Ethari a concrete institutional cost, such as losing authority, research access, or mission resources;
- differentiate safety and research objections;
- compress NaMo’s explanation of books, recordings, and methods if the visual evidence has already established them.

### Scene 7 — Memory Core Choice

Strengths:

- backup introduces identity continuity as a physical decision;
- NaMo chooses risk rather than safe duplication;
- `บางครั้งไม่ต่างค่ะ` is concise.

Revision actions:

- retain almost unchanged;
- verify technology terms against the glossary;
- ensure the core is not framed as a soul container.

### Scene 8 — Descent

Strengths:

- archival lesson becomes a self-correction, not a posthumous conversation;
- navigation loss creates immediate action;
- the signal gives the episode a forward objective.

Revision actions:

- clearly mark the archival source as playback;
- retain the manual landing choice;
- avoid turning the spoken lesson into a polished recap.

### Scene 9 — Ruined City

Strengths:

- strong environmental storytelling;
- the child signal creates urgency;
- source loss forces continued action without certainty.

Revision actions:

- retain minimal dialogue;
- consider removing `ตรวจพบผู้ใช้งาน` if the action already communicates recognition;
- protect the silence after disconnection.

### Scene 10 — Contact

Strengths:

- name pronunciation echoes EP1 without recreating Khru Ba;
- the child controls access;
- NaMo closes the lesson log rather than narrating it.

Revision actions:

- preserve the child’s practical instruction;
- reduce observation-list density if the episode has already stated all three points;
- retain NaMo walking downhill as the final action.

## Priority Revision List for EP4 v2.1

### Required

1. Reduce repeated explanation of recovery versus restoration.
2. Differentiate Ethari and the other researchers through objective and risk.
3. Give Ethari a visible institutional cost for the Earth mission.
4. Preserve the voice-model refusal without repeating EP3 verbatim.
5. Clarify archival playback versus generated speech.

### Recommended

1. Keep alien environments restrained and shootable.
2. Segment long visual descriptions for screenplay readability.
3. Verify memory-core, recovery, and identity terminology against the technology glossary.
4. Preserve all ten scenes and the low-power signal ending.

## Proposed Revision Scope

Allowed:

- dialogue compression;
- character-voice differentiation;
- action-line segmentation;
- one visible consequence for Ethari;
- terminology clarification.

Not authorized:

- changing 143 years or 31% recovery;
- opening the synthetic voice model;
- resurrecting Khru Ba;
- changing the Earth-return decision;
- adding supernatural identity transfer;
- changing the final contact signal.
