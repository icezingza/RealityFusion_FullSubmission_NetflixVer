## Required review output format

Every specialist review should identify:

1. reviewer name or role;
2. relevant qualification or professional background;
3. screenplay version reviewed;
4. episode / scene references;
5. finding category: `ACCEPTABLE`, `REVISE`, `CLARIFY`, or `OUTSIDE REVIEWER SCOPE`;
6. concise rationale;
7. proposed wording or staging correction when appropriate;
8. whether the issue is factual, legal/regulatory, ethical, cultural, clinical, technical, or production-related;
9. whether the correction changes Canon or only execution detail.

## Canon guardrails for all reviewers

## Completion gate

A domain moves from `PENDING EXTERNAL REVIEW` to `REVIEWED` only when a traceable review record exists and cites the exact screenplay version reviewed.

A screenplay correction prompted by specialist feedback is not considered complete until:

- the change is applied to a new controlled draft version;
- Canon impact is checked;
- the relevant specialist finding is linked or cited in the revision record;
- the current-draft manifest is updated.
