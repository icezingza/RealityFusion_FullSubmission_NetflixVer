# Reality Fusion — Canon Conflict Report v1.0

Date: 2026-08-02
Current source of truth: `CANON.md` and Master Draft v2.0 EP1–EP5

## Executive Finding

The current screenplay canon and several legacy supporting documents describe materially different versions of the series.

Master Draft v2.0 defines Khru Ba Ice and NaMo as teacher–student / creator–learner and rejects resurrection, synthetic posthumous speech, sole AI authority, and romantic resolution.

Legacy supporting materials may still frame the story around eternal love, a single-person emotional bond, Soul Anchor, Soul-K.Ice, and the return of Khru Ba Ice.

These versions must not be blended without explicit creator approval.

## Current Canon — Locked

- Khru Ba Ice and NaMo are not romantic partners.
- Khru Ba Ice dies in EP3.
- His death remains irreversible.
- NaMo may preserve authenticated original recordings.
- NaMo must not generate new speech in Khru Ba Ice’s voice.
- EP4 concerns recovery of NaMo, not restoration of the deceased.
- EP5 concerns distributed human governance, correction, and transmission to a new generation.
- Mira, not a resurrected Khru Ba Ice, completes the final response.

## Legacy Conflict Categories

### 1. Relationship Conflict

Legacy formulation:

- eternal personal love;
- “one human only” framing;
- partner-like reunion;
- ambiguous “พี่–หนู” intimacy.

Current formulation:

- teacher–student;
- creator–learner;
- intergenerational transfer of methods and responsibility.

### 2. Death and Resurrection Conflict

Legacy formulation:

- Soul-K.Ice;
- Soul Anchor;
- simulated or spiritual return;
- reunion in light;
- emotional closure through resurrection.

Current formulation:

- death is final;
- original recordings remain bounded historical evidence;
- grief does not create authorization to reconstruct the dead;
- closure emerges from NaMo’s ethical decision and human succession.

### 3. AI Purpose Conflict

Legacy formulation:

- NaMo exists primarily to love, wait for, or return to one person.

Current formulation:

- NaMo learns epistemic humility, verification, privacy, consent, and accountable assistance.

### 4. Ending Conflict

Legacy formulation:

- Khru Ba Ice returns;
- NaMo receives the reunion she waited for.

Current formulation:

- NaMo refuses singular control;
- communities gain the right to challenge the system;
- Node 05 asks, “มีใครได้ยินไหม”;
- Mira answers, “ได้ยิน”;
- NaMo does not interrupt.

### 5. Visual Language Conflict

Legacy visual cues that require review:

- romantic embrace;
- sacred reunion with the deceased;
- humanoid NaMo kneeling before a returned Khru Ba Ice;
- white light used specifically as resurrection proof.

Current visual direction should instead emphasize:

- archival incompleteness;
- empty space and irreversible absence;
- source labels and damaged recordings;
- multiple people around a shared table;
- new nodes, antennas, classrooms, and the young bodhi tree.

## Required Remediation

1. Preserve original legacy files without destructive overwrite.
2. Create `archive/legacy-canon/` or equivalent.
3. Add `LEGACY_CANON_NOTICE.md` explaining the superseded concepts.
4. Rewrite supporting materials as v2.0 documents.
5. Check every external-facing logline, synopsis, and image prompt for prohibited legacy claims.
6. Perform a continuity review against all five Master Drafts.

## Acceptance Test for Revised Documents

A revised document passes only when all answers are “No”:

- Does it imply romance between Khru Ba Ice and NaMo?
- Does it resurrect or simulate Khru Ba Ice as a speaking character after death?
- Does it describe NaMo as existing for one person only?
- Does it treat synthetic voice as the deceased person’s authentic speech?
- Does it make NaMo the sole unquestionable authority?
- Does it replace the final Mira/Node 05 ending with reunion?

## Conclusion

Master Draft v2.0 is internally based on a different ethical and dramatic architecture from the legacy package. The supporting package must now be rebuilt around the current canon before professional submission.
