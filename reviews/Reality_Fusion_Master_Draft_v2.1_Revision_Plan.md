## Purpose

## Approved Canon Correction

### EP3 character-name conflict

Problem:

- EP3 Scene 2 introduces a thirteen-year-old student named `มิรา`.
- Current Canon and Continuity Bible reserve `มิรา / Mira` for the survivor child who first appears in EP5, 143 years later.
- Using the same distinctive name in both periods creates an avoidable continuity ambiguity.

Approved action:

- Rename the EP3 student from `มิรา` to `พิม` in all EP3 appearances.
- Preserve the character’s age, actions, dialogue, function, and relationship to the other students.
- Reserve `มิรา / Mira` exclusively for the EP5 survivor character.

Affected EP3 scenes:

- Scene 2 — outdoor source-verification lesson
- Scene 5 — hospital visit
- Scene 7 — governance handoff
- Scene 8 — archive preparation
- Scene 11 — funeral and authenticated recording
- Scene 12 — failed network contact
