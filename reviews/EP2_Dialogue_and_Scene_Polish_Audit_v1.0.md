## Purpose

This audit identifies dialogue, action-line, pacing, character-voice, exposition, privacy, and continuity changes required before creating EP2 Master Draft v2.1. It does not overwrite Master Draft v2.0.

## Episode-Level Verdict

EP2 has a strong external conflict and a clear escalation: a misleading clip spreads, the school suspends access, investigators request full logs, Khru Ba refuses to expose student records, the child who edited the clip takes responsibility, and NaMo learns to separate a harmful act from a complete identity.

The strongest material is action-based: the speaker being muted, Khru Ba refusing to sign, consent language on screen, the deleted post that has already spread, the child recording a correction, anonymized review stations, NaMo changing from a student code to the name `นนท์`, and the final governance notebook.

The main risk is excessive conceptual clarity. Several exchanges state the privacy, compassion, responsibility, and memory distinctions in finished language. The v2.1 pass should preserve the ethical structure while making the dialogue more resistant, interrupted, and situational.

## Character Voice Check

### Khru Ba Ice

Current voice is mostly aligned: concise, dry, practical, and willing to accept responsibility.

Required refinements:

- shorten completed philosophical definitions;
- allow him to answer indirectly or redirect with a task;
- retain technical and consent language only when tied to an immediate decision;
- do not make every answer sound like a quotable principle.

### NaMo

Current voice is appropriately formal and procedural.

Required refinements:

- keep her focused on authority, export scope, incident ownership, and classification;
- reduce moments where she explains mature emotional consequences too fluently;
- let learning appear through changed system behavior: redaction, naming, questions, and refusal to reduce a person to an incident.

### Nont / นนท์

He has a credible adolescent rhythm: impulsive, ashamed, indirect, and action-first.

Required refinements:

- preserve his failed first apology recording;
- avoid giving him a polished public confession;
- let the correction remain imperfect and visibly less successful than the original clip.

### School Director

He should remain risk-averse, procedural, and concerned with institutional exposure.

Required refinement:

- avoid making him a generic antagonist;
- preserve a credible reason for requesting records, but make the breadth of the request clearly contestable.

### Science Teacher

He functions best as a skeptical educator rather than an anti-technology spokesperson.

Required refinement:

- keep his questions specific and testable;
- avoid repeated abstract prompts that turn the hearing into a philosophy seminar.

### Parent Representative

Her concern is authority and social order.

Required refinement:

- retain her discomfort, but give her language that sounds personal and practical rather than institutional.

## Privacy and Safeguarding Finding

The episode correctly rejects indiscriminate disclosure. However, the student message suggesting possible self-harm introduces a separate safeguarding duty that is not resolved by confidentiality alone.

For v2.1:

- do not expose the child to reviewers or the public;
- show, or explicitly preserve evidence of, a restricted escalation path to a qualified adult;
- avoid implying that only Khru Ba and the system should retain the message indefinitely without a safeguarding response;
- this point requires qualified child-safeguarding and mental-health review before production lock.

This is a production-risk note, not a diagnosis or a claim that the current scene is legally incorrect.
