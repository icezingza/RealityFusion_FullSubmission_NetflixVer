## Purpose

This audit identifies dialogue, action-line, pacing, character-voice, exposition, governance, and ending risks before creating EP5 Master Draft v2.1. Master Draft v2.0 remains unchanged.

## Episode-Level Verdict

EP5 resolves the series coherently. NaMo does not restore a global network by fiat, does not reactivate Khru Ba as synthetic authority, and does not answer the final signal before Mira. The strongest material is action-led: Mira controls entry, the validation question unlocks the archive, NaMo lowers her hand from `YES`, selects `CONFIGURE GOVERNANCE`, chooses original audio, joins a table without a head seat, and remains silent when Node 05 calls.

The principal risk is procedural exposition. Scenes 4–6 contain many explicit governance statements, while Scenes 8 and 10 restate the voice-model distinction already established in EP3 and EP4. The revision should preserve every ethical decision while allowing conflict, scarcity, and character behavior to carry more meaning.

## Character Voice Check

### NaMo

Precise, formal, and increasingly economical. Preserve her refusal and silence. Avoid letting her become the most eloquent speaker in every governance exchange.

### Mira

Direct, skeptical, concrete, and impatient with status. Her strongest lines are short: `กดสิ`, `ก็จริง`, `ผ่าน`, `ยังไม่รู้`, `ได้ยิน`.

Required refinement:

- protect her blunt rhythm;
- avoid giving her polished thematic speeches;
- keep her authority grounded in testing and action.

### Elderly Man

He needs a credible operational reason for central authority beyond impatience. His community has survived for generations with scarce power, fragmented knowledge, and uncertain contact; those costs should be visible in his decisions.

### Community Voices

The emergency, dissent, privacy, and resource concerns should come from people with distinct practical stakes, not generic debate roles.

## Scene-by-Scene Audit

### Scene 1 — Following the Signal

Strengths:

- strong post-collapse geography;
- yellow plastic avoids false religious symbolism;
- the route is discovered through action.

Revision actions:

- retain minimal dialogue;
- remove or simplify `เหมือนเครื่องหมายทาง` if visual staging makes it obvious;
- preserve uncertainty about the signal source.

### Scene 2 — Entry Test

Strengths:

- Mira controls the encounter;
- humor comes from practical suspicion;
- the original recording establishes provenance and distrust of authority.

Revision actions:

- name Mira only when the community names her later, preserving the reveal;
- keep the adults wary without making them faceless guards;
- retain the question-as-password turn.

### Scene 3 — Validation and Archive

Strengths:

- EP1’s uncertainty lesson becomes access control;
- the elderly man distinguishes waiting for NaMo from waiting for infrastructure;
- the locked core creates a concrete objective.

Revision actions:

- retain the validation question;
- consider shortening the archived Khru Ba line if it repeats a later recording;
- keep the elderly man’s focus on system recovery.

### Scene 4 — Root Identity

Strengths:

- NaMo physically stops before restoring;
- central authority is offered as a functional shortcut;
- the manual survives as an alternative.

Revision actions:

- reduce the complete explanation `ถ้าดิฉันตอบแทนทุกคน...` toward a more immediate operational objection;
- make the cost of delay visible through power, medical, food, or communications pressure already present in the community;
- give the elderly man a concrete reason for needing rapid restoration;
- preserve the lowered hand and manual pickup.

### Scene 5 — The Right to Challenge

Strengths:

- the manual contains many generations of handwriting;
- Mira proves the community already practices uncertainty;
- `CONFIGURE GOVERNANCE` is the episode’s decisive turn.

Revision actions:

- retain `ถ้าคุณตอบมั่ว หนูปิดเครื่องแน่`;
- consider cutting NaMo’s question about whether the elderly man challenged his teacher if it feels didactic;
- let Mira’s prior contradiction and the handwritten rule establish the same point visually.

### Scene 6 — Governance Meeting

Strengths:

- rules arise from actual failure modes;
- emergency authority and minority objection are included;
- children’s right to question is consistent with the series.

Revision actions:

- highest exposition concentration in EP5;
- convert at least one rule into a disputed action or access request;
- distinguish speakers by material stakes;
- preserve temporary emergency authority, audit, local consent, and challenge rights;
- consider showing the system refuse activation until two communities confirm rather than explaining every rule verbally.

### Scene 7 — Network Connection

Strengths:

- Khru Ba’s recording is authentic and finite;
- NaMo admits uncertainty about her state;
- Mira’s response is ordinary rather than therapeutic.

Revision actions:

- preserve `อ๋อ` and the chair action;
- consider shortening the recording to avoid another full legacy statement;
- retain the slow connection percentages.

### Scene 8 — Voice Archive Choice

Strengths:

- temptation is explicit;
- NaMo admits that she wants to hear the model;
- Mira independently chooses original audio.

Revision actions:

- this repeats established explanation from EP3 and EP4;
- preserve the temptation and choice, but compress the technical explanation;
- retain `อยากค่ะ` because it prevents the refusal from feeling easy;
- avoid turning Mira into an instant ethics expert.

### Scene 9 — Learning Center

Strengths:

- knowledge, repair work, and source checking share one space;
- there is no throne or religious branding claim;
- the elderly man’s silent observation shows partial change.

Revision actions:

- retain as visual payoff;
- keep dialogue short;
- consider giving the elderly man one practical contribution to demonstrate shared governance.

### Scene 10 — Young Bodhi Tree

Strengths:

- Mira asks the voice question in plain language;
- NaMo distinguishes memory from invented speech;
- the scene returns to work and class.

Revision actions:

- avoid restating the complete ethical distinction after Scene 8;
- compress NaMo’s response and let Mira’s `ก็ปล่อยให้เขาพูดเท่าที่พูดไว้` close the issue;
- preserve watering and departure as the final actions.

### Scene 11 — Final Classroom and Node 05

Strengths:

- no one sits at the head of the table;
- `ยังไม่รู้` transfers from NaMo to Mira;
- `ANSWER: PENDING` preserves openness;
- Mira answers Node 05 while NaMo remains silent.

Revision actions:

- the lesson log states the series conclusion very fully; consider reducing observations from three to one or two;
- preserve `ใครสอนใคร` only if it does not over-explain the ending;
- do not add dialogue after `ได้ยิน`;
- NaMo’s silence is the final action and must remain.

## Priority Revision List for EP5 v2.1

### Required

1. Reduce procedural exposition in Scenes 4–6.
2. Give the elderly man a concrete survival-based argument for central restoration.
3. Turn at least one governance rule into an immediate action or system constraint.
4. Compress repeated voice-model explanation in Scenes 8 and 10.
5. Reduce the final lesson log while preserving Mira’s answer and NaMo’s silence.

### Recommended

1. Distinguish community voices through resources and responsibilities.
2. Preserve Mira’s short, blunt vocabulary.
3. Keep governance contestable, local, and auditable.
4. Preserve all eleven scenes and the Node 05 ending.

## Proposed Revision Scope

Allowed:

- dialogue compression;
- practical conflict additions within existing scenes;
- action-line segmentation;
- community-voice differentiation;
- reducing lesson-log text.

Not authorized:

- NaMo restoring the network alone;
- resurrection or synthetic return of Khru Ba;
- romance;
- changing Mira’s role;
- changing the final Node 05 message;
- adding dialogue after Mira says `ได้ยิน`.
