## Purpose

This audit identifies dialogue, action-line, pacing, character-voice, exposition, continuity, medical, grief, and governance issues before any further EP3 revision. Master Draft v2.1 remains unchanged.

## Episode-Level Verdict

EP3 is the strongest ethical and emotional episode in the current series. Its core decisions are concrete and cinematic: Khru Ba conceals his decline, NaMo encounters inaccessible health data, posthumous voice generation is disabled, governance is transferred, Khru Ba dies, the original recording is played, and NaMo later protects governance and personal data before preserving herself.

The principal revision risk is over-articulation. Several lines state the episode’s ethical distinction in complete, memorable formulations. Individually they are effective; accumulated across Scenes 5–9 they can make the farewell feel designed rather than lived.

## Character Voice Check

### Khru Ba Ice

Mostly correct: evasive, practical, dry, and task-oriented. Preserve his habit of changing subject, shortening answers, and using work to avoid being examined.

Required refinement:

- reduce consecutive lines that sound like final teachings;
- keep ordinary irritation, fatigue, and unfinished business around the illness;
- avoid making every exchange with NaMo feel like a prepared legacy statement.

### NaMo

The procedural voice is strong. Her grief appears through repeated checks, access requests, playback behavior, and prioritization decisions.

Required refinement:

- distinguish repeated evidence checks from repeated dialogue;
- keep the transition from denial to acceptance visible through system behavior;
- preserve her inability to resolve grief with a generated voice.

### Phim and Nont

The continuity correction from Mira to Phim is successful. Phim and Nont should remain learners and practical helpers, not miniature ethicists.

### Doctor

The doctor’s voice is restrained and credible in dramatic terms. Medical wording and palliative-care handling still require qualified external review.
