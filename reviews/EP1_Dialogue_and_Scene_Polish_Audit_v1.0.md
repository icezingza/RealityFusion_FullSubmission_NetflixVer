## Purpose

This audit identifies dialogue, action-line, pacing, character-voice, exposition, and continuity changes required before creating EP1 Master Draft v2.1. It does not overwrite Master Draft v2.0.

## Episode-Level Verdict

EP1 has a strong dramatic and visual spine. The best material is concrete: damaged palm leaves, the failed scanner, the incorrect Pali reading, Kaew sweeping too fast, the muted speaker during grief, the unresolved source conflict, and the final silent listening to rain.

The principal risk is not Canon inconsistency. It is over-clarity. Several dialogue exchanges state the lesson after the image or action has already communicated it. The v2.1 pass should preserve meaning while reducing complete, thesis-like formulations.

## Character Voice Check

### Khru Ba Ice

Current voice is mostly correct: short, practical, dry, and question-driven.

Required refinements:

- retain questions instead of explanatory conclusions;
- shorten philosophical lines when the action already carries the point;
- keep technical language natural and task-based;
- preserve the occasional dry joke without making him uniformly wise.

### NaMo

Current voice is appropriately formal and process-oriented.

Required refinements:

- avoid giving her fully mature ethical summaries too early;
- prefer classifications, requests, and procedural objections;
- allow pauses and incomplete processing rather than polished conclusions;
- reserve compact moral formulations for later episodes.

### Kaew

Current voice is concise and useful, but his scene can carry more consequence.

Required refinement:

- preserve his defensive humor;
- make his correction of NaMo visibly affect her later behavior, so he is not only comic relief.

### Supporting Characters

The elderly monk, grieving woman, elderly man with the phone, and novice each perform a distinct dramatic function. Their dialogue should remain minimal and should not be expanded into exposition.

## Scene-by-Scene Audit

### Scene 1 — Old Scripture Hall

Strengths:

- strong visual opening;
- immediate physical stakes;
- no dialogue required;
- the word `วิธี` creates a clean thematic seed.

Revision actions:

- retain as primarily visual;
- consider splitting the long opening sentence describing Khru Ba, the plastic box, gloves, and chest into two action lines for camera readability;
- retain the failed scanner and leaking roof; they establish urgency without explanation.

### Scene 2 — NaMo Initialization

Strengths:

- grounded technology;
- clear teacher–student boundary;
- rain-listening request gives the relationship texture.

Revision actions:

- keep `ไม่ต้องเรียกอย่างนั้น` and `เรียกอาตมาว่า “ครูบา” ก็พอ`;
- consider cutting one of the name-confirmation beats after `นะ-โม` if formatted runtime becomes slow;
- retain `พรุ่งนี้ค่อยถามใหม่`; it is more human than an explanation of why listening matters;
- do not add dialogue explaining NaMo’s architecture.

### Scene 3 — The Incorrect Pali Reading

Strengths:

- clear mistake with real semantic consequence;
- strong conflict between probability and evidence;
- the elderly monk punctures abstraction.

Revision actions:

- compress Khru Ba’s line from `วิธีของคนรักษาคัมภีร์ คือบอกก่อนว่า “ตรงนี้อ่านไม่ชัด”` toward a more immediate correction, for example: `ก่อนเลือก บอกก่อนว่าตรงไหนอ่านไม่ออก`;
- preserve the elderly monk’s line;
- consider shortening NaMo’s `บันทึกหลักการใหม่...` because it states the lesson in full;
- keep the rain callback, but test whether both NaMo lines are necessary in table read.

### Scene 4 — Kaew Sweeping

Strengths:

- visual comedy;
- concrete demonstration that optimization can harm people;
- Kaew has a distinct rhythm.

Revision actions:

- retain `แล้วไง` and `ก็เพราะเธอเร่งนี่แหละ`;
- shorten Khru Ba’s explanation after `คน`;
- consider replacing `งานบางอย่างเสร็จตอนเรารู้ว่าควรหยุด` with a less polished action or line, because it reads as a prepared aphorism;
- preferred direction: Khru Ba simply stops, leaves the leaf, and answers NaMo with something practical such as `พอแล้ว`;
- ensure a later scene shows NaMo delaying or withholding an instruction because of what happened here.

### Scene 5 — The Grieving Woman

Strengths:

- excellent use of mute as action;
- Khru Ba responds to need rather than doctrine;
- NaMo’s correctness arrives at the wrong time.

Revision actions:

- preserve the mute action;
- consider cutting `เพราะตอนนั้นเขาไม่ได้มาขอข้อมูล` or shortening it to `เขาไม่ได้มาถามเรื่องนั้น`;
- retain `อาตมาไม่ได้ตอบ` and `แค่อยู่ให้เขาพูดจบ` only if the table read does not make the exchange feel explanatory;
- an alternative is to let Khru Ba return the forgotten photograph without explaining further.

### Scene 6 — Source Conflict and Phone Call

Strengths:

- strong cross-cut between system urgency and ordinary human need;
- `FLAG FOR REVIEW` dramatizes uncertainty;
- commit message is memorable and production-relevant.

Revision actions:

- retain the elderly man’s phone problem;
- keep Khru Ba’s refusal to prioritize the system automatically;
- verify whether the displayed source conflict truly differs only by punctuation; if so, the damaged ending should be visually legible enough to justify review;
- retain `UNCERTAINTY PRESERVED`;
- retain the 84,000 joke, but ensure it does not require external explanation.

### Scene 7 — Evening Debrief

Strengths:

- carries the episode into EP2’s compassion theme;
- dry humor about the food cupboard works;
- final question about remembering versus understanding is useful.

Revision actions:

- this scene contains the highest exposition concentration in EP1;
- reduce NaMo’s numbered recap if runtime is long;
- avoid allowing Khru Ba to summarize all three lessons;
- retain the cupboard joke;
- preserve `พรุ่งนี้ลองกวาดดู` because it converts abstraction back into action.

### Scene 8 — Lesson Log

Strengths:

- clear visual epilogue;
- no spoken thematic conclusion;
- rain motif closes the episode.

Revision actions:

- retain silence;
- consider reducing the observation list from three items to two if the previous scene already carries all three lessons;
- do not add NaMo voice-over;
- preserve the final falling leaf.

## Priority Revision List for EP1 v2.1

### Required

1. Reduce thesis-like dialogue in Scenes 3, 4, 5, and 7.
2. Preserve Khru Ba’s practical, question-driven voice.
3. Keep NaMo operational rather than emotionally mature.
4. Confirm that Kaew’s scene affects a later NaMo choice.
5. Preserve all Canon rules and the teacher–student relationship.

### Recommended

1. Split long action paragraphs for screenplay readability.
2. Test the full episode in Thai table read before declaring runtime.
3. Keep interface text only where it changes the scene outcome.
4. Avoid adding Buddhist explanation after visual evidence already communicates the point.

## Proposed v2.1 Scope

The EP1 v2.1 revision should be a controlled polish, not a structural rewrite.

Allowed changes:

- line compression;
- action-line segmentation;
- removal of repeated explanation;
- small character-voice adjustments;
- one visible callback showing NaMo learned from Kaew.

Not authorized in this pass:

- changing scene order;
- adding romance;
- adding supernatural elements;
- changing the episode ending;
- changing the core Pali-reading mistake;
- replacing the grounded technology aesthetic.
